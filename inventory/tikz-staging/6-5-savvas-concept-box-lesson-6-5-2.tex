\textbf{CONCEPT: Properties of Logarithms}\par\vspace{2pt}
\small For positive numbers $b$, $m$, and $n$ with $b\ne 1$, the following properties hold.\par\vspace{8pt}
\normalsize
\begin{tabular}{@{} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4.7cm} >{\centering\arraybackslash}p{4.3cm} @{}}
\toprule
Product Property of Logarithms & $\log_b(mn)=\log_b m+\log_b n$ \\[6pt]
Quotient Property of Logarithms & $\log_b\left(\dfrac{m}{n}\right)=\log_b m-\log_b n$ \\[6pt]
Power Property of Logarithms & $\log_b(m^{n})=n\log_b m$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
