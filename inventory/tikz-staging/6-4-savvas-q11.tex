\textbf{Error Analysis: Find the inverse of }$f(x)=5^{x-6}+2$\par\vspace{8pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{} >{\centering\arraybackslash}p{4.5cm} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4.5cm} @{}}
\toprule
$y=5^{x-6}+2$ & Write in $y=f(x)$ form. \\
$x=5^{y-6}+2$ & Interchange $x$ and $y$. \\
$x-2=5^{y-6}$ & Subtract 2 from each side. \\
$y-6=\log_5 x-2$ & Rewrite in logarithmic form. \\
$y=\log_5 x-2+6$ & Add 6 to each side. \\
$y=\log_5 x+4$ & Simplify. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\par\vspace{8pt}
$f^{-1}(x)=\log_5 x+4$
