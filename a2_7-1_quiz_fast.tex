\noindent{\LARGE\textbf{7-1 Lesson Quiz, short form: answer key}}

\vspace{4pt}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.6pt}

The numbers are the Savvas item numbers (TE printed page 353A). The TE prints no standards codes; the skill codes are the Realize ``Skills Review and Practice'' codes from the TE item analysis.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{B, C, E.} The terminal side is $30^\circ$ above the negative $x$-axis, so the angle is $180^\circ-30^\circ=150^\circ$. B: $150^\circ-360^\circ=-210^\circ$. C: $\tfrac{5\pi}{6}\cdot\tfrac{180^\circ}{\pi}=150^\circ$. E: $150^\circ+360^\circ=510^\circ$. Distractors: A, $-\tfrac{4\pi}{3}=-240^\circ$, is coterminal with $120^\circ$, not $150^\circ$; D, $210^\circ$, is in Quadrant III. Savvas item 1, DOK 1, skill F84 (Examples 1 and 3). \textbf{Bonus 5:} B, C and E with no extra box; 3 for two correct boxes and none wrong; 0 otherwise.
\setcounter{enumi}{2}
\item \textbf{$112^\circ$; $285^\circ$; $202^\circ$.} Quadrant II: $180^\circ-68^\circ=112^\circ$. Quadrant IV: $360^\circ-75^\circ=285^\circ$. Quadrant III: $180^\circ+22^\circ=202^\circ$. Savvas item 3, DOK 1, skill F84 (Example 1, Parts A and B). \textbf{10 points:} 3 per blank, plus 1 for writing the subtraction or addition for at least one blank (Example 1 writes each angle as $180^\circ\pm$ or $360^\circ-$ the reference angle).
\item \textbf{$30^\circ$; $300^\circ$.} $\tfrac{\pi}{6}\cdot\tfrac{180^\circ}{\pi}=30^\circ$; $\tfrac{5\pi}{3}\cdot\tfrac{180^\circ}{\pi}=\tfrac{5\cdot180^\circ}{3}=300^\circ$. Savvas item 4, DOK 1, skill F85 (Example 3A). \textbf{10 points:} 5 each, of which 2 for the work (multiplying by $\tfrac{180^\circ}{\pi}$).
\end{enumerate}
