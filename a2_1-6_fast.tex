\wspart{Example 1}{Solve a system of linear equations}
A \textbf{system}: two equations, same variables. Its \textbf{solution}: the pair $(x,y)$ that makes \emph{both} true.

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.5\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
Solve $\begin{cases} x+2y=3\\ x-2y=4\end{cases}$ \quad\textbf{Substitution}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, itemsep=1pt]
\item Solve each equation for $x$:\\ $x=3-2y$ \quad and \quad $x=4+2y$
\item Both equal $x$, so set them equal:\\ $3-2y=4+2y$
\item Solve: $-1=4y$, so $y=-\tfrac14$
\item Substitute back: $x=3-2\!\left(-\tfrac14\right)=\tfrac72$
\item Solution: $\left(\tfrac72,\,-\tfrac14\right)$
\end{enumerate}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\textbf{Elimination} (same system)
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, itemsep=1pt]
\item The $y$ terms are $+2y$ and $-2y$. Add the equations:\\
$(x+2y)+(x-2y)=3+4$
\item $2x=7$, so $x=\tfrac72$
\item Substitute into $x+2y=3$:\\ $\tfrac72+2y=3$, so $2y=-\tfrac12$, $y=-\tfrac14$
\item Same solution: $\left(\tfrac72,\,-\tfrac14\right)$
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Check} in both equations:\\
$\tfrac72+2\!\left(-\tfrac14\right)=\tfrac72-\tfrac12=3$ \checkmark\\
$\tfrac72-2\!\left(-\tfrac14\right)=\tfrac72+\tfrac12=4$ \checkmark
\end{minipage}

\vspace{-6pt}\textbf{Say it:} $(x,y)=\blank[2cm]$ works in both equations because \lines[2]\vspace{-12pt}

\wspart{Example 2}{Solve a system of linear inequalities}
Malcolm mows lawns for \$20 per hour and walks dogs for \$10 per hour. He wants to earn \textbf{at least} \$200 each week, he can work \textbf{at most} 20 hours, and he must walk dogs \textbf{at least} 5 hours. How many hours of each job meet his goals?

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.5\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\textbf{Step 1} Define the variables.\\
$x=$ hours mowing lawns, \ $y=$ hours walking dogs

\textbf{Step 2} Write an inequality for each constraint.
\begin{itemize}
\item earn at least \$200: \ $20x+10y\ge 200$
\item at most 20 hours: \ $x+y\le 20$
\item at least 5 hours of dogs: \ $y\ge 5$
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Step 3} Graph each boundary line ($\ge$ and $\le$: \textbf{solid}). Test a point to pick the side to shade:
\begin{itemize}
\item $20(0)+10(0)\ge200$? False: shade \emph{away} from $(0,0)$.
\item $0+0\le20$? True: shade toward $(0,0)$.
\item $y\ge5$: $(8,8)$ is true, shade above $y=5$.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Step 4} The \textbf{solution region} is where all the shadings overlap (the shaded triangle).

\textbf{Check a point.} $(12,7)$ is in the region:\\
$20(12)+10(7)=310\ge200$ \checkmark\\
$12+7=19\le20$ \checkmark \qquad $7\ge5$ \checkmark\\
So 12 h mowing and 7 h walking dogs meets every goal.
\end{minipage}\hfill
