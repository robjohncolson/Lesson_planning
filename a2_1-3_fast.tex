\wspart{Example 3}{Write a piecewise-defined rule from a graph}
\begin{minipage}[c]{0.34\textwidth}
\begin{tikzpicture}
\begin{axis}[sheetgrid]
\addplot[very thick, blue] coordinates {(-2,-10) (1,-1)};
\addplot[very thick, red] coordinates {(1,4) (5,8)};
\addplot[very thick, green!60!black] coordinates {(5,8) (8,8)};
\node[closed] at (axis cs:-2,-10) {};
\node[open] at (axis cs:1,-1) {};
\node[closed] at (axis cs:1,4) {};
\node[closed] at (axis cs:5,8) {};
\node[closed] at (axis cs:8,8) {};
\node[blue, font=\scriptsize\bfseries, right] at (axis cs:0.4,-3) {A};
\node[red, font=\scriptsize\bfseries, above left] at (axis cs:3,6) {B};
\node[green!60!black, font=\scriptsize\bfseries, above] at (axis cs:6.5,8) {C};
\end{axis}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[c]{0.64\textwidth}
Three pieces, so three intervals. Each piece is a line, so find a slope and one point, then solve for $b$.

\vspace{2pt}
\small
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Piece A} & \textbf{Piece B} & \textbf{Piece C} \\ \hline
interval $-2\leq x<1$ & interval $1\leq x\leq 5$ & interval $5<x\leq 8$ \\
point $(-2,-10)$, $m=3$ & point $(1,4)$, $m=1$ & point $(5,8)$, $m=0$ \\
$y=mx+b$ & $y=mx+b$ & $y=mx+b$ \\
$-10=3(-2)+b$ & $4=1(1)+b$ & $8=0(5)+b$ \\
$b=-4$ & $b=3$ & $b=8$ \\
$f(x)=3x-4$ & $f(x)=x+3$ & $f(x)=8$ \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{minipage}

\vspace{2pt}
The rule is \quad
$f(x)=\begin{cases}3x-4, & -2\leq x<1\\ x+3, & 1\leq x\leq 5\\ 8, & 5<x\leq 8\end{cases}$
\hfill \parbox[t]{0.5\textwidth}{\small Why $x<1$ and not $x\leq1$ for Piece A? The point $(1,-1)$ is \textbf{open}; $x=1$ belongs to Piece B, where the point $(1,4)$ is \textbf{closed}. Each $x$ gets exactly one piece.}

\textbf{Say it:} Piece A is the line with slope \blank[1.5cm] on the interval \blank[2.5cm]. Piece B is the line with slope \blank[1.5cm] on the interval \blank[2.5cm]. Piece C is \blank[6cm].
\lines[3]

\wspart{Example 5}{Graph a step function}
The shipping cost $y$ of an online order depends on the weight $x$ of the items. Graph the function, then state the domain and range and what they represent. Each $y$-value goes with a whole \textbf{interval} of $x$-values, so each piece is a horizontal segment, \textbf{open} on the left and \textbf{closed} on the right. The graph looks like a staircase: a piecewise function made only of horizontal pieces is a \textbf{step function}.

\noindent\hfil\begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Weight of items (lb)} & $0<x\leq 2$ & $2<x\leq 4$ & $4<x\leq 6$ & $6<x\leq 8$ \\ \hline
\textbf{Shipping cost (\$)} & 5 & 8 & 11 & 14 \\ \hline
\end{tabular}\hfil\par
