\textbf{Example 6 -- Cube vs Cylinder (guided)}

\vspace{0.2em}
A company is comparing two package designs. The more efficient design has the \textbf{smaller} ratio of surface area to volume ($SA/V$).

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.75]
  % Cube (Option 1) -- side 2x
  \draw (0,0) rectangle (1.6,1.6);
  \draw (0,1.6)--(0.4,2.0);
  \draw (1.6,1.6)--(2.0,2.0);
  \draw (1.6,0)--(2.0,0.4);
  \draw (0.4,2.0)--(2.0,2.0);
  \draw (2.0,2.0)--(2.0,0.4);
  \draw[dashed] (0,0)--(0.4,0.4);
  \draw[dashed] (0.4,0.4)--(2.0,0.4);
  \draw[dashed] (0.4,0.4)--(0.4,2.0);
  \node at (0.8,-0.3) {\small $2x$};
  \node at (2.3,1.0) {\small $2x$};
  \node[font=\bfseries] at (0.8,-1.0) {Option 1: Cube};

  % Cylinder (Option 2) -- radius x, height 2x  --  shifted further right
  \begin{scope}[shift={(7,0)}]
    \draw (-0.8,0)--(-0.8,1.8);
    \draw (0.8,0)--(0.8,1.8);
    \draw (0,1.8) ellipse (0.8 and 0.25);
    \draw (-0.8,0) arc (180:360:0.8 and 0.25);
    \draw[dashed] (-0.8,0) arc (180:0:0.8 and 0.25);
    \draw[<->] (0,1.8) -- (0.8,1.8); \node[above] at (0.4,1.8) {\small $x$};
    \draw[<->] (1.1,0) -- (1.1,1.8); \node[right] at (1.1,0.9) {\small $2x$};
    \node[font=\bfseries] at (0,-1.0) {Option 2: Cylinder};
  \end{scope}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.6em}
{\small\itshape We will work the \textbf{cube} together. Then you will compute the \textbf{cylinder} on your own.}

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Option 1 -- Cube} \quad (we do)\par\smallskip
$V = (2x)^3 = 8x^3$ \qquad $SA = 6(2x)^2 = 24x^2$
\[
\frac{SA}{V} \;=\; \frac{24x^2}{8x^3} \;=\; \frac{3}{x}
\]

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent\textbf{Option 2 -- Cylinder} \quad (your turn)\par\smallskip
Use $V = \pi r^2 h$ and $SA = 2\pi r h + 2\pi r^2$, with $r = x$ and $h = 2x$.

\vspace{0.3em}
$V = $ \framedline{4in}

\vspace{0.3em}
$SA = $ \framedline{4in}

\vspace{0.3em}
$\dfrac{SA}{V} = $ \framedline{4in}

\vspace{0.6em}
\textbf{Comparison.}\; Both ratios should come out to \framedline{1in}. What does that tell you about the design choice?\\
{\small\itshape Frame: ``The ratios are \frameblank, so the company should choose based on \frameblank\ (cost, manufacturing, etc.)''}\\
