\noindent{\LARGE\textbf{6-6 Lesson Quiz, short form: answer key}}

\vspace{4pt}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.6pt}

Question numbers are the Savvas Lesson Quiz numbers (TE printed page 327A). Realize Skills Review and Practice codes are from the TE quiz item analysis; the TE prints no standards codes.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Q\arabic*.}, start=2]
\item \textbf{C, $\tfrac23$ hour; 4.} Common base 2: $4^{3x-1}=(2^2)^{3x-1}=2^{6x-2}$ and $8^x=(2^3)^x=2^{3x}$, so $6x-2=3x$ and $x=\tfrac23$. Volume: $8^{2/3}=4$ (and $4^{3(2/3)-1}=4^1=4$). Savvas item 2, DOK 2, skills F72, F73. Example 1. \textbf{Bonus 5:} 3 for C, 2 for the common-base work; a bare letter earns 3.
\item \textbf{Part A:} line 2 = Rewrite with a common base; line 3 = Power of a Power Property; line 4 = Property of Equality for Exponential Equations. \textbf{Part B: line 3; $x=7$.} $\left(5^2\right)^{x-2}=5^{2(x-2)}=5^{2x-4}$, not $5^{2x-2}$; then $2x-4=x+3$, $x=7$. Check: $5^{10}=25^{5}$. Savvas item 3 (3A and 3B), DOK 3, skills F72, F73. Example 1; the packet's Practice is its structural parallel. \textbf{10 points:} Part A 4 (all three labels; 2 for two), Part B 6 (3 for line 3, 3 for $x=7$ with work).
\item \textbf{A and B ($x=1$, $x=4$).} Power Property: $\log x^2=\log(5x-4)$, so $x^2=5x-4$, $x^2-5x+4=0$, $(x-1)(x-4)=0$. Both make every argument positive ($x>0$ and $5x-4>0$): $x=1$ gives $0=0$; $x=4$ gives $\log16=\log16$. C does not solve it; D and E make $\log x$ undefined. Savvas item 4, DOK 2, skills F72, F73. Example 5. \textbf{10 points:} 6 for exactly A and B (3 for one correct and none wrong), 4 for the work with the check.
\end{enumerate}
